A field crew often shares one laptop. Each person can be an operator on it with their own settings, and UXDuck records who did what.

## Operators

The operator you are acting as is at the foot of the sidebar. Choose it to switch to another operator, or choose **New operator…** to add one. **Settings › Operators** lists them, with rename and remove in each row's menu.

## What is whose

- **Each operator's own:** units and formats, map choices, shortcuts, telemetry tiles, saved layouts, chats with the AI, and their account sign-in. Signed in, these follow them to their other computers.
- **The computer's:** links, vehicles, live telemetry, recordings, each vehicle's parameters, missions and history, and running simulations. Everyone at the computer sees the same vehicles.

## Signing in

An operator who isn't signed in is a guest on this computer. Switching to another person signs the previous account out, so the next person never uses someone else's account; sign in again when you switch back.

## Switching while flying

Release your controllers first, including keyboard control. Until then UXDuck refuses the switch and says so. Telemetry and recordings carry on through the switch.

## Who did it

Each action keeps the name of the operator who did it, as it was then: renaming or switching later never changes it.

- **Logbook** lists every flight on the computer with who flew it, and its operator filter shows one operator's entries and totals. **Your logbook**, in the operator menu, opens it on yours.
- A vehicle's **History** shows each parameter change and command an operator made with their name, such as `BATT_CAPACITY 3300 → 4000 · Ben`.
