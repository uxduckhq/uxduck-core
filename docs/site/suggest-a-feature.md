Suggest features from the chat.

## From the chat

Ask the agent for something UXDuck does not do yet, such as airspeed for a rover. It does what it can, then offers once to suggest it to the UXDuck team. Say yes and it writes the suggestion as a card in the chat. The agent never sends it: read it, change anything, and choose **Send**, or **Cancel**.

Nothing is attached unless you tick it: **Technical details**, or **This chat**. Sent anonymously unless you tick **Credit me with a username and tell me when it ships**. If you're signed out, **Tell me when it ships** tells you once you update to the release that has it.

## Your username

Credited suggestions show your username and nothing else about you. Choose it the first time you are credited, or in **Settings › Account**, or with `uxduck account username`. It is 3 to 20 lower-case letters, digits, `-` and `_`.
