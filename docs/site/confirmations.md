UXDuck never stops you with a question. Commands that could hurt someone or the vehicle are pressed and held instead.

## Click or hold

- **Click:** modes, return to launch, land, take off and going to a place.
- **Press and hold:** arming and disarming, rebooting, and forcing arm or disarm, on the ground and in flight alike. These buttons have a ring before their name that fills while you hold; they act when it is full. A quick click does nothing and says "Press and hold".

From a shortcut or the action list, a command you hold opens on its own with its button ready: hold **Space** until it fills, or press **Esc** to cancel.

When a vehicle refuses to arm or disarm, the Control tab says why and shows **Force arm** or **Force disarm** to hold.

When a vehicle is offline, the Control tab says so once. Commands still go to its last known address and may arrive late.

## Send it to a place

On the Control tab, press **Go to** (or **G**), set the height, then click the map: the vehicle goes there. Each click sends it again, until you press **G**, **Esc** or **Stop**. You can also type a latitude and longitude and press Enter.

## Approvals

When an agent asks to send a command, you approve it once. **Settings › Approvals** lists what you always allowed, each with **Reset**.
