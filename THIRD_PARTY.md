third-party licenses or notices.

- `crates/uxduck-mavlink/src/dialect/ardupilotmega.rs` is generated by UXDuck's
  tooling from the MAVLink definitions distributed with `mavio`. The upstream
  message definitions are MIT-licensed. See `licenses/MAVLink-COPYING.txt` and

  Apache-2.0 WITH LLVM-exception, with only its zstd dependency updated. See its

  export. Their package manifests and license files govern those components.
  `Cargo.lock` pins the versions. Binary distributors must preserve the notices
  required by the dependencies they distribute.

  downloads, not relicensed or bundled into this source export. ArduPilot's
  applicable GPL terms continue to apply to those downloads. Test parameter
  fixtures in `crates/uxduck-contracts/fixtures/pdef/` use UXDuck-authored wording.
