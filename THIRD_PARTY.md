The Apache-2.0 license covers UXDuck's own contributions. It does not replace
third-party licenses or notices.

- `crates/uxduck-mavlink/src/dialect/ardupilotmega.rs` is generated by UXDuck's
  tooling from the MAVLink definitions distributed with `mavio`. The upstream
  message definitions are MIT-licensed. See `licenses/MAVLink-COPYING.txt` and
  [MAVLink's licensing explanation](https://github.com/mavlink/mavlink-devguide/blob/master/en/index.md#license).
  The SDK also generates Rust message types from those definitions during builds.
- `crates/uxduck-mavlink/px4/events.json` is taken by `scripts/px4-events.py`
  from the events catalogue PX4 1.17.0 builds (`etc/extras/all_events.json`):
  its health and arming check messages. PX4 is BSD-3-Clause licensed. Its
  licence is beside the file, in `crates/uxduck-mavlink/px4/LICENSE`.
- Native controller input includes `gilrs` 0.11.2 and `gilrs-core` 0.6.8 under
  `vendor/`, with bounded macOS attachment/removal and Linux initial-state patches documented in
  `vendor/gilrs/UXDUCK-PATCH.md`. Their upstream MIT and Apache-2.0 licenses are
  retained in each directory. The bundled SDL controller database retains its
  own license at `vendor/gilrs/SDL_GameControllerDB/LICENSE`.
- Other Cargo dependencies are downloaded separately, not vendored in this source
  export. Their package manifests and license files govern those components.
  `Cargo.lock` pins the versions. Binary distributors must preserve the notices
  required by the dependencies they distribute.
- Native and browser ArduPilot simulators and parameter catalogues are separate
  downloads, not relicensed or bundled into this source export. ArduPilot's
  applicable GPL terms continue to apply to those downloads. Test parameter
  fixtures in `crates/uxduck-contracts/fixtures/pdef/` use UXDuck-authored wording.
